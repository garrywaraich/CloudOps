Mentor Challenge

Before moving into advanced topics like scopes, existing resources, and module registries, I would strongly recommend completing Scenarios 1-10. If you can build those from memory without looking at Microsoft Learn, your Bicep fundamentals will be much stronger than most candidates applying for junior Cloud Engineer roles.
